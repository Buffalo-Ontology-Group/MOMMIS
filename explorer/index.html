Built from your research

The MOMMIS conceptual model, MilkOligoDB identifier layer and evidence-integration framework inform this prototype.

What this version does

Search a small study-record dataset, inspect provenance, keep local curation notes, and export structured records. The ontology view reads OWL classes and definitions through the backend, or a clearly labeled offline snapshot. Evidence filters query the supplied study records; this pilot does not run full OWL reasoning or SPARQL.

What remains to build

Domain-reviewed extractions, ontology term alignment, explicit supporting and opposing evidence, and a shared curation workflow. No live Drive synchronization or clinical recommendation engine is connected.
