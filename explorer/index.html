How to interpret these clinical links

Growth and tolerability in infants with allergy are not proof of allergy resolution. Immune markers are not clinical diagnoses. EVC001, Bi-26 and a three-species product are distinct interventions. These studies do not demonstrate a complete 2′-FL → B. infantis → disease-prevention pathway.

Safety context for preterm probiotic studies: the FDA has warned of invasive infection, including a fatal case. This research explorer is not a treatment recommendation. FDA safety communication ↗
